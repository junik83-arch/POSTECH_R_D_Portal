> 김종신 교수는 포항공과대학교(POSTECH)에서 신경면역학 및 뇌신경장벽 연구실(Lab of Neuroimmunology and CNS barriers, NICB)을 이끌고 있으며, 연구실은 C5 바이오헬스케어센터에 위치합니다. 연구 주제는 뇌와 망막 및 그 장벽(수막/두개골, BBB/BRB, 맥락얼기, RPE/맥락막, 안구 표면)에서 신경-면역-혈관 간 상호작용이 항상성과 질환에 어떻게 기여하는지를 규명하는 것으로, 중추신경계 면역관용과 면역특권, 자가면역·염증·감염(다발성경화증, 포도막염, 알츠하이머병, 수막염·뇌염·망막염), 장벽과 체액(뇌척수액, 방수)의 면역 niche 등 세 축으로 진행됩니다. 유전자조작 마우스 모델, 공초점 및 생체 내 이광자 현미경, 다색 유세포분석, 시공간적 단일세포 시퀀싱 등을 활용합니다. 대표 성과로는 YAP/TAZ의 혈관신생 및 혈관장벽 성숙 조절 연구(J Clin Invest, 2017; BRIC 선정 의학 분야 우수성과)와 액틴 리모델링 인자의 섬모형성 조절 연구(Nat Commun, 2015)가 있으며, 최근에는 광의학·신경면역 상호작용 관련 공동연구에 교신저자로 참여하고 있습니다.

Research
Last update: 2023-06-20
We study immune responses and interactions between neuronal, glial,
endothelial
and immune cells in CNS and its barriers. Drawing from the fields of immunology, vascular biology, and neurobiology, we use state-of-art techniques of genetically engineered mouse models, confocal microscopy, intravital two-photon microscopy, multicolor flow cytometry, and spatiotemporal single-cell sequencing to mechanistically understand immune regulation and dysregulation during CNS homeostasis and diseases. We focus on three major areas of research:
CNS immune tolerance and privilege
Decode the mechanisms governing CNS (brain and retina) immune tolerance and privilege in the CNS and its barriers and associated lymphoid organs
CNS autoimmunity, inflammation, and infections
Unravel
the roles of microglia, border-associated macrophages, dendritic cells, T cells, and endothelial cells and their interactions in CNS autoimmunity (multiple sclerosis, uveitis),
inflammation (Alzheimer's disease),
and infections (meningitis, encephalitis, and retinitis)
CNS barriers and fluids
Characterize the immunological niches in CNS barriers (meninges, skull choroidal plexus, RPE/choroid, BBB/BRB, and ocular surface) and fluids (CSF, aqueous humor) and identify their roles in regulating immune homeostasis and inflammation in the CNS

Graduate Student Applicants
Students who wish to pursue graduate studies in our laboratory are required to complete
at least three months of research participation before applying to the graduate program
. This may include
SURF, WURF
, and/or an additional
research internship
, for a total research period of at least three months. Candidates are then evaluated based on their research performance, potential, and overall suitability for graduate study in the laboratory.
Prospective students are encouraged to contact Prof. Jongshin Kim with the following materials:
Curriculum vitae
Academic transcript
A brief statement of research interests and motivation for joining the laboratory
English proficiency score, if available
Prospective research interns should contact us at least three months before their intended start date.
Postdoctoral Applicants
Prospective postdoctoral researchers are encouraged to contact Prof. Jongshin Kim with a CV, a brief summary of previous research and future research interests, and contact information for three references.
We are recruiting  graduate students, postdocs, and undergrad research interns!
