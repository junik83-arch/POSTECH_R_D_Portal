## 연구키워드
Public health monitoring, Patient-provider collaboration, Personal Informatics, Self-tracking, Technology for health and wellbeing

## 주요성과
- Empirical study to examine multi-stakeholder perception of an LLM chatbot for public health monitoring
- Empirical study to examine the impact of long-term memory integration into an LLM chatbot for public health monitoring
- Empirical study to examine whether and how LLM chatbots fulfill public agencies' expectations toward AI
- Development and evaluation of a clinical decision support tool to deveop a longitudinal care plans in Psychiatry

As a human-computer interaction (HCI), computer-supported cooperative work (CSCW), and Health researcher, I examine how health monitoring technology can better account for infrastructural complexity of clinical and public health care. My research approach encompasses understanding how stakeholders use existing health monitoring tools through interviews, surveys, and log analysis; developing prototypes to explore novel design ideas; and evaluating these designs through real-world field deployments.
