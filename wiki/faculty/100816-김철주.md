> 김철주(Cheol-Joo Kim) 교수는 POSTECH 화학공학과 부교수로, Nano Emerging Materials and Hybrid Devices Lab(CJ Kim Group)을 이끌고 있습니다. POSTECH 신소재공학과에서 학사(2006)와 박사(2011) 학위를 받은 뒤 코넬대학교 화학·화학생물학과에서 박사후연구원(2012-2016)을 거쳐 2017년 POSTECH 조교수로 부임했고 2022년부터 부교수로 재직 중입니다. 연구실은 화학기상증착을 이용한 2차원 소재의 대면적 성장(에피택시, 결함·준안정상 설계), 원자 수준 구조 공학, 신규 나노소재 물성 탐색, 2D 반도체·분자 하이브리드 소자 등 신개념 나노소자 개발을 주요 연구 주제로 삼고 있습니다. 대표 성과로는 적층결함 없는 층별 에피택시를 가능하게 하는 반데르발스 계면 연구(ACS Nano, 2025), 단층 결정의 경사 결정립계 제어(Nano Lett., 2023), 2차원 전자소재의 결정립계 공학 리뷰(Adv. Mater., 2023) 등이 있으며, Science에 게재 승인된 모듈형 반데르발스 MOSFET 논문에도 공저자로 참여했습니다. 수상 이력으로는 2023년 한국그래핀학회 신진과학자 학술상, 2012년 한국연구재단 차세대 연구자 육성 펠로십 등이 있습니다.

About | New Beginnings
top of page
About
We are an interdisciplinary research group in the Department of Chemical Engineering at POSTECH. We develop innovative platforms for designing nanomaterials at the atomic scale through growth, assembly, and chemical processing. We study the fundamental properties of artificial materials with the aid of state-of-the-art characterization tools and fabricate emerging nanodevices with advanced functionalities.
Growths of 2D Materials
We grow 2D materials with nanoscale uniformity over large areas using chemical vapor deposition processes to provide key elements for nanotechnology. In addition to the epitaxial growth of highly crystalline materials, we aim to grow materials with designed disorders and thermodynamically metastable phases by developing new growth techniques.
Atomic-scale Structural Engineering
Arbitrary control of material structures with atomic-scale precision lies at the heart of nanoelectronics and photonics, encompassing interface engineering for devices, the development of metamaterials, and the artificial construction of new materials. We aim to develop new toolsets with additional structural degrees of freedom to enable designs beyond the capabilities of current techniques.
Discovery of
Nano-material Properties
Emerging nanomaterials can offer novel and enhanced physical and chemical properties that bulk materials do not. We explore these properties by studying materials fabricated in our lab. Our goals involve discoveries of new high-mobility semiconductors, ultrathin glass with exceptional mechanical toughness, and chemically active electronic and optical materials with high sensitivity and selectivity.
Emerging
Nano Devices
While emerging nanomaterials can be integrated with existing device platforms to enable novel architectures, techniques customized for the target materials must be developed to achieve optimum device performance. We develop such techniques for specific nanomaterials and realize non-conventional nanodevices, such as atomically thin field-effect transistors, electronic molecular sensors, and devices based on ion transport.

Nano Emerging Materials and Hybrid Devices Lab.
CJ Kim Group
Our Mission
Nanoscale materials, whose functionalities arise from their unique atomic configurations and small sizes, lie at the heart of addressing a wide range of engineering challenges. Our mission is to develop versatile and scalable techniques to artificially design materials with atomic-level precision—surpassing the capabilities of current state-of-the-art methods. We investigate novel properties in these materials and explore their potential applications in emerging technologies.
