> 이대수 교수는 인공 복합산화물 연구실(Artificial Complex Oxide Lab, ACOL)을 이끌고 있으며, 홈페이지에 따르면 연구실은 포스텍(POSTECH)으로 이전될 예정이라고 안내되어 있습니다. 연구 초점은 복합 산화물(특히 전이금속 기반) 에피탁셜 박막으로, 전하·스핀·격자·궤도 자유도의 상호작용을 단일 원자층 수준에서 제어해 기존에 없던 물성과 기능성을 구현하는 데 있으며, 강유전성, 플렉소전기성, 스핀-오빗로닉스, 초전도를 주요 연구 분야로 제시하고 있습니다. 대표 성과로는 교신저자로 발표한 Science 논문 "Emergence of room-temperature ferroelectricity at reduced dimensions"(2015)와 "Isostructural metal-insulator transition"(2018), Nature Communications의 "Colossal flexoresistance in dielectrics"(2020) 및 "Stabilizing hidden room-temperature ferroelectricity via a metastable atomic distortion pattern"(2020), 그리고 최근의 Nature Physics "Flexoelectric polarizing and control of a ferromagnetic metal"(2024)과 Nature Nanotechnology "Ferroelastic writing of crystal directions in oxide thin films" 등이 있습니다. 연구실은 RHEED를 갖춘 펄스레이저증착(PLD) 챔버 3기, 엑시머 레이저, 원자힘현미경, 강유전 특성 평가용 TF analyzer, 산소 분위기 튜브 퍼니스 등 박막 합성·분석 장비를 보유하고 있습니다. 또한 연구에 의욕적인 학부생과 대학원생의 참여를 상시 환영한다고 밝히고 있습니다.

◽ Pioneering the Future of Complex Oxide Epitaxial Thin Films
◽
At ACOL, our journey begins at the frontier of condensed matter physics & materials science. We dive deep into the realm of complex oxide epitaxial thin films, a class of materials holding promises that have the potential to reshape our technological landscape.
◽
Why Complex Oxides?
◽
Complex oxides, particularly those based on transition metals, are a treasure trove of multifaceted properties. These materials can exhibit a rich variety of physical behaviors, from ferroelectricity and magnetism to superconductivity and unconventional transport. Such diverse characteristics stem from the interplay of charge, spin, lattice, and orbital degrees of freedom at the atomic level. It's no wonder that these materials have garnered attention for their potential to revolutionize everything from computing to energy storage.
◽ Elevating the Paradigm ◽
But we're not just about understanding these properties; we're about mastery. Our research is not confined to merely tuning the inherent traits of these oxides. Instead, we embark on a more audacious journey — controlling these materials at the single atomic layer level. This meticulous level of manipulation allows us to envision and create materials and functionalities that have never before been realized in the world. We are pushing the boundaries of what's possible by synthesizing new forms, conjuring up unprecedented properties, and harnessing their potential in next-generation applications.
◽ Diverse Frontiers:
F
erro- &
F
lexoelectricity to
S
pin-orbitronics &
S
uperconductivity
◽
Our exploration begins with the intriguing world of
ferroelectricity
. These materials, possessing spontaneous electric polarization that can be reversed by an external electric field, hold great potential in non-volatile memory devices and advanced sensing applications.
Venturing further, we delve into the realm of
flexoelectricity
. This intriguing phenomenon arises when mechanical asymmetries produce electric polarization. Such properties pave the way for innovative applications in energy harvesting and nano-electromechanical systems.
One of our pivotal research avenues is
spin-orbitronics
. By understanding and controlling the complex interplay between an electron's spin and its orbital motion, we uncover groundbreaking prospects in data storage and quantum computing. Harnessing this synergy in our epitaxial films might be the key to realizing ultrafast, low-energy electronics, bridging the present to a quantum future.
Lastly, our passion for understanding and utilizing unique properties has led us to investigate
superconductivity
in complex oxides. The promise of zero electrical resistance at remarkably high temperatures not only challenges our understanding of condensed matter physics but also opens doors to revolutionary applications like quantum computing, efficient energy transmission, and next-generation magnetic levitation transport.
Through each of these frontiers, we aim not just to understand, but to redefine the boundaries of what's possible in condensed matter physics & materials science.
◽
A Vision for Tomorrow
◽
At ACOL, we believe that the materials of tomorrow will be defined by our ability to craft, control, and comprehend them at atomic precisions. Our investigations into complex oxide epitaxial thin films are not just about exploring new frontiers; they're about creating them. We invite you to join us on this exhilarating journey as we sculpt the future, one atomic layer at a time.
