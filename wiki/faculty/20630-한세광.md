> 한세광 교수는 POSTECH 신소재공학과 소속으로 생체의료나노소재연구실(Biomedical Nanomaterials Lab, BNL)을 이끌고 있으며, 바이오센서, 바이오이미징, 약물전달시스템, 재생의료, 헬스케어 웨어러블 기기 등 스마트 바이오소재 기반 나노메디신 연구에 집중하고 있습니다. Nature Reviews Materials, Nature Photonics, Nature Communications, Science Advances, Advanced Materials, ACS Nano 등에 130편 이상의 논문을 발표했고 국내외 특허 120건 이상을 출원했으며, 최근에도 신축성 바이오전자소자용 금속 나노와이어-필름 구조, 광화학적 조직 접합용 발광 패치, 간질성 방광염 치료용 자기구동 마이크로젤봇 등의 연구를 보고했습니다. 산학협력 성과로는 LG생명과학과의 서방형 인성장호르몬(Declage), 메가젠과의 인공골이식재(BonePlus), 신풍제약과의 수술 후 유착방지제(Medicurtain), PHI BioMed와의 EGF 코스메디컬(PHI-EGF) 상용화 기여가 있으며, 삼성미래기술육성재단 지원으로 유비쿼터스 헬스케어용 스마트 콘택트렌즈를 개발했습니다. 학술 활동으로는 Biomacromolecules, ACS Biomaterials Science and Engineering, Applied Sciences 편집위원과 Biomaterials Research 부편집장을 맡고 있고, 2012년 하버드 의과대학 방문교수를 지냈으며 국가과학기술자문회의 위원으로 활동하고 있습니다. 수상 이력으로는 2015년 대통령표창, 2017년 보건복지부 장관상, 2013년 교육부 장관상이 있습니다.

About Lab
Biomedical Nanomaterials Lab (BNL) is Prof. Sei Kwang Hahn’s laboratory in the Department of Materials Science and Engineering (DMSE) at the world class research university, POSTECH. BNL is working for smart biomaterials to develop nanomedicines including biosensors, bio-imaging systems, drug delivery systems, regenerative medicines and healthcare wearable devices.
Prof. Hahn published more than 130 journal papers including Nature Reviews Materials, Nature Photonics, Nature Communications, Science Advances, Progress in Polymer Science, Advanced Materials, and ACS Nano, and filed more than 120 Korean and international patents. He is one of the Editorial Board Members for Biomacromolecules (American Chemical Society), ACS Biomaterials Science and Engineering, and Applied Sciences, and the Associate Editor of  Biomaterials Research. He won the Korean President Award in 2015, the Minister of Health & Welfare Award in 2017, and the Korean Minister of Education Award in 2013. He was a visiting professor at the Harvard Medical School in 2012. He is a member of National Science and Technology Council.
BNL has greatly contributed to the successful commercialization of SR-hGH (Declage) in collaboration with LG Lifesciences, artificial bone graft (BonePlus) with MegaGen, post-surgical adhesion prevention solution (Medicurtain) with ShinPoong Pharmaceuticals and EGF cosmedicals (PHI-EGF) with PHI BioMed. Recently, BNL has developed smart contact lenses for ubiquitous healthcare supported by the Samsung Futuristic Technology Foundation. Prof. Hahn worked as a consultant for Johnson & Johnson in New Jersey and made a collaboration contract with Hoffman-La Roche. He is the CEO of a bio-venture company of “PHI BioMed” founded in 2014 and the Outside Director of KISWIRE. He was a member of Samsung Future Technology Committee and  the National Science and Technology Council, and is a member of National Academy of Engineering Korea.

Biography
Sei Kwang Hahn is the Seok Cheon Endowed Chair Professor in the Department of Materials Science and Engineering at Pohang University of Science and Technology (POSTECH). He obtained his B.S. (1991), M.S. (1993) and Ph.D. (1996) in the Department of Chemical and Biomolecular Engineering at Korea Advanced Institute of Science and Technology (KAIST). As the youngest Ph.D. at LG Chemical Group in 1996, he started his research for the commercial development of the sustained release formulation of hGH under the trade name of Declage?. After that, he did his post-doctoral research with Prof. Allan Hoffman in the Department of Bioengineering at the University of Washington for 2001-2002. He worked at the Hoffman-La Roche group, Chugai Pharmaceutical Co. in Japan for 2002-2005. After joining at POSTECH in 2005, he worked as a consultant for Johnson & Johnson in New Jersey in 2008, a visiting professor at Harvard Medical School and Massachusetts General Hospital for 2012-2013, at Stanford University for 2019-2020, and at the University of Tokyo and iCONM for 2026-2027. He was the Outside Director of Kiswire Co. for 2020-2026 and is the founder and CEO of PHI BIOMED Co., one of the National Innovative Enterprise 1000 nominated by the Korean Minister of SMEs and Startups. He won the Science and Technology Medal of Korean President in 2024, the Best National Convergence Technology Award in 2023, the prestigious Medical Industry Grand Prize and Song-Gok Science & Technology Award in 2022, the Controlled Release Society Award in 2018, the Minister of Health and Welfare Award in 2017, and the Korean President Award in 2015. He was the Samsung Future Technology Committee for 2016-2018 and the Presidential Advisory Council on Science and Technology for 2017-2019. He has been elected as the National Academy of Engineering of Korea in 2020, the Fellow of the International Union of Societies for Biomaterials Science and Engineering (IUSBSE) in 2024, and the Fellow of Korean Academy of Science and Technology (KAST) in 2025, and the Fellow of the Controlled Release Society in 2026. He has been elected to the Vice President for the Polymer Society of Korea in 2024 and for the Korean Society of Biomaterials in 2025-2026. He published more than 210 journal papers including Nature Reviews Materials, Nature Photonics, Nature Communications, Science Advances, Progress in Polymer Science, and Advanced Materials, and filed more than 170 Korean and international patents. He has worked as the Editor-in-Chief of Biomaterials Research, Executive Editor of Advanced Drug Delivery Reviews, Associate Editor of Biomaterials and Heliyon, the Guest Editor of Advanced Materials, Advanced Drug Delivery Reviews, Biomaterials, and Journal of Controlled Release, and the Editorial Board Member of Advanced Drug Delivery Reviews, ACS Biomaterials Science and Engineering, ACS Applied Bio Materials, Biomacromolecules, Advanced Nanobiomed Research and so on.
Research
Research Topics - Biomaterials for Nanomedicine
The convergence of recent advances in nanotechnology with modern biology and medicine has created the new research domain of nanobiotechnology. The use of nanobiotechnology in medicine is termed nanomedicine. Nanomedicine research includes targeted cancer therapies, localized drug delivery, improved cell material interactions, the development of diagnostics for rapid monitoring, scaffolds for tissue engineering, and gene delivery systems. Successful research and development in nanomedicine where ultimately patients can benefit from these new technologies require the interaction of a multitude of disciplines including material science and engineering, cellular biology and clinical translational research.

About Lab
Biomedical Nanomaterials Lab (BNL) is Prof. Sei Kwang Hahn’s laboratory in the Department of Materials Science and Engineering (DMSE) at the world class research university, POSTECH. BNL is working for smart biomaterials to develop nanomedicines including biosensors, bio-imaging systems, drug delivery systems, regenerative medicines and healthcare wearable devices.
Prof. Hahn published more than 130 journal papers including Nature Reviews Materials, Nature Photonics, Nature Communications, Science Advances, Progress in Polymer Science, Advanced Materials, and ACS Nano, and filed more than 120 Korean and international patents. He is one of the Editorial Board Members for Biomacromolecules (American Chemical Society), ACS Biomaterials Science and Engineering, and Applied Sciences, and the Associate Editor of  Biomaterials Research. He won the Korean President Award in 2015, the Minister of Health & Welfare Award in 2017, and the Korean Minister of Education Award in 2013. He was a visiting professor at the Harvard Medical School in 2012. He is a member of National Science and Technology Council.
BNL has greatly contributed to the successful commercialization of SR-hGH (Declage) in collaboration with LG Lifesciences, artificial bone graft (BonePlus) with MegaGen, post-surgical adhesion prevention solution (Medicurtain) with ShinPoong Pharmaceuticals and EGF cosmedicals (PHI-EGF) with PHI BioMed. Recently, BNL has developed smart contact lenses for ubiquitous healthcare supported by the Samsung Futuristic Technology Foundation. Prof. Hahn worked as a consultant for Johnson & Johnson in New Jersey and made a collaboration contract with Hoffman-La Roche. He is the CEO of a bio-venture company of “PHI BioMed” founded in 2014 and the Outside Director of KISWIRE. He was a member of Samsung Future Technology Committee and  the National Science and Technology Council, and is a member of National Academy of Engineering Korea.

Biography
Sei Kwang Hahn is the Seok Cheon Endowed Chair Professor in the Department of Materials Science and Engineering at Pohang University of Science and Technology (POSTECH). He obtained his B.S. (1991), M.S. (1993) and Ph.D. (1996) in the Department of Chemical and Biomolecular Engineering at Korea Advanced Institute of Science and Technology (KAIST). As the youngest Ph.D. at LG Chemical Group in 1996, he started his research for the commercial development of the sustained release formulation of hGH under the trade name of Declage?. After that, he did his post-doctoral research with Prof. Allan Hoffman in the Department of Bioengineering at the University of Washington for 2001-2002. He worked at the Hoffman-La Roche group, Chugai Pharmaceutical Co. in Japan for 2002-2005. After joining at POSTECH in 2005, he worked as a consultant for Johnson & Johnson in New Jersey in 2008, a visiting professor at Harvard Medical School and Massachusetts General Hospital for 2012-2013, at Stanford University for 2019-2020, and at the University of Tokyo and iCONM for 2026-2027. He was the Outside Director of Kiswire Co. for 2020-2026 and is the founder and CEO of PHI BIOMED Co., one of the National Innovative Enterprise 1000 nominated by the Korean Minister of SMEs and Startups. He won the Science and Technology Medal of Korean President in 2024, the Best National Convergence Technology Award in 2023, the prestigious Medical Industry Grand Prize and Song-Gok Science & Technology Award in 2022, the Controlled Release Society Award in 2018, the Minister of Health and Welfare Award in 2017, and the Korean President Award in 2015. He was the Samsung Future Technology Committee for 2016-2018 and the Presidential Advisory Council on Science and Technology for 2017-2019. He has been elected as the National Academy of Engineering of Korea in 2020, the Fellow of the International Union of Societies for Biomaterials Science and Engineering (IUSBSE) in 2024, and the Fellow of Korean Academy of Science and Technology (KAST) in 2025, and the Fellow of the Controlled Release Society in 2026. He has been elected to the Vice President for the Polymer Society of Korea in 2024 and for the Korean Society of Biomaterials in 2025-2026. He published more than 210 journal papers including Nature Reviews Materials, Nature Photonics, Nature Communications, Science Advances, Progress in Polymer Science, and Advanced Materials, and filed more than 170 Korean and international patents. He has worked as the Editor-in-Chief of Biomaterials Research, Executive Editor of Advanced Drug Delivery Reviews, Associate Editor of Biomaterials and Heliyon, the Guest Editor of Advanced Materials, Advanced Drug Delivery Reviews, Biomaterials, and Journal of Controlled Release, and the Editorial Board Member of Advanced Drug Delivery Reviews, ACS Biomaterials Science and Engineering, ACS Applied Bio Materials, Biomacromolecules, Advanced Nanobiomed Research and so on.
Research
Research Topics - Biomaterials for Nanomedicine
The convergence of recent advances in nanotechnology with modern biology and medicine has created the new research domain of nanobiotechnology. The use of nanobiotechnology in medicine is termed nanomedicine. Nanomedicine research includes targeted cancer therapies, localized drug delivery, improved cell material interactions, the development of diagnostics for rapid monitoring, scaffolds for tissue engineering, and gene delivery systems. Successful research and development in nanomedicine where ultimately patients can benefit from these new technologies require the interaction of a multitude of disciplines including material science and engineering, cellular biology and clinical translational research.
