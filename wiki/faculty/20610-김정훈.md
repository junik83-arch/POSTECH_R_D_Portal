- Identification of dopamine-mediated regulation of learned fear expression in amygdala inhibitory circuits
- Identification of cell adhesion molecules as determinants for synaptic plasticity at the circuit levels
- Functional roles of phospholipase Cγ1 in forebrain for manic-like behaviors
- Visualization and quantification of microRNA in single cells
