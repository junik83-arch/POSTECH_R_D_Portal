> 유선철 교수는 POSTECH 극한환경로봇연구실(HERO Lab, Hazardous and Extreme Environment Robotics Lab, 2008년 설립)을 이끄는 지도교수로, 필드 로보틱스와 인공지능, 센싱·인지 기술을 활용해 재난 현장, 수중, 원자력 발전소와 같은 극한환경에서 사람을 대체할 수 있는 로봇 기술을 연구합니다. 주요 연구 분야는 호버링형 자율무인잠수정(AUV) 'Cyclops'를 활용한 수중 스캐닝·3차원 복원·다중로봇 임무 수행, 수중 소나 및 전기장 기반 위치추정과 매핑(SLAM), 생체모방형 수중로봇, 계단·배관·협소 통로 등 비정형 원전 환경을 위한 궤도형·사족보행 로봇의 고기동 제어 등입니다. 수행 과제로는 포스텍에 설치된 한국수력원자력의 PKRC(발전소 및 위험환경 작업·검사 전용 로봇 개발 연구센터, 2024.06~2027.05, 60억 원), 국방기술진흥연구소의 UUV용 수중 SLAM 기술 개발, 국토교통과학기술진흥원의 다목적 해상 부유식 인프라 건설기술 개발, 원전 격납 용기 균열 정밀 안전검사 로봇시스템 개발 등이 있습니다. 연구 성과로는 보행·유영·기어다니기가 가능한 생체모방 다중모드 수중로봇 'HERO-BLUE'를 다룬 IEEE Transactions on Robotics 논문을 비롯해 다수의 국제·국내 학술논문과 수중 3차원 모델 획득 기법 등 43건의 특허가 있으며, 학부 Creative IT Design III와 대학원 Introduction to Intelligent Robotics 강의를 담당하고 국립해양과학관 전문가 특별강연 등 사회 공헌 활동도 수행했습니다.

Field Robotics & Sensor System
We focus on researching robots capable of autonomously performing missions in extreme environments such as disaster situations, underwater environments, and nuclear power plants. We develop robot's hardware and software, then evaluate performance through experiments in real-world environments. Currently, we are researching and developing robots capable of completing missions and performing various tasks in extreme environments through techniques including biomimetic robotic designs and multi-robot systems.
Cyclops
A hovering-type AUV called Cyclops was developed for sensing and scanning in shallow water. Using Cyclops, we are conducting various research including underwater scanning, underwater 3D reconstruction, and mission execution with multiple robots.
High-Mobility Robot Control for Unstructured Nuclear Environments
This study aims to develop control algorithms that maximize robot mobility in complex and unstructured environments within nuclear power plants, such as stairs, pipes, and narrow passageways. By utilizing tracked robots and quadruped walking robots, we research stable posture control and dynamic obstacle avoidance technologies, and validate the feasibility of mission performance in rough terrains resembling actual nuclear facilities. In particular, we are focusing on achieving optimal path planning and environment-adaptive locomotion technologies through hybrid mobility mechanisms.
Active Link Buoyancy Control
Underwater manipulation robots utilizing ALBC (Active Link Buoyancy Control) systems offer an innovative underwater 3D posture control mechanism. With its novel concept and design hold significant potential for facilitating advanced underwater missions, including object recovery and 3D object reconstruction.
HERO-BLUE
"HERO-BLUE," a novel hybrid bio-inspired robot platform, integrates swimming and legged motions. HERO-BLUE operates successfully in various real-world environments through the utilization of state-of-the-art control strategies.
Underwater Multi-Robots System (Cyclops - Agent)
Multi-robot system for performing precision work for an extended period of time in harsh underwater environments. A main robot (Cyclops) carries an agent robot to a working area and controls it to serve precision work.
Wall-Climbing Robot System
Dual-unit wall-climbing robot can be attached to and maneuver on surfaces while traversing obstructions. Therefore, the inspection of high ceiling or surfaces are possible, especially on extreme environments such as nuclear power plants.
Biomimetic Underwater Robot for Inspection of Marine Structures (BRIM)
A biomimetic underwater robot, called BRIM, is capable of walking, obstacle negotiation, and swimming for precise inspections of marine structures. Current research focuses on reinforcement learning-based control methods to enhance its operational efficiency.
LiDAR-Stereo Thermal Sensor Fusion for Indoor Disaster Environment
This study focuses on generating reliable point clouds by fusing stereo thermal cameras with LiDAR sensors in indoor foggy environments due to disasters. The proposed method enables the creation of point clouds even in dense indoor foggy settings and can be applied to existing LiDAR mapping algorithms.
Underwater Real Time Microscope
Our underwater microscope aims to observe plankton in real-time in a stable manner, by using hatch mechanism to stabilize the water flow during observation. This system was verified for continuous long-term underwater operation for monitoring phytoplanktons.
Field AI & Perception
We conduct research aimed at developing cutting-edge AI technologies and applying robot AI in the field robotics to enhance robot perception, intelligence, and automation capabilities. Our research spans across all areas of robotics, including robot AI for camera vision, sonar, active sensors, and control strategies.
AI research themes include enhancing underwater ultrasonic camera perception, reinforcement learning for underwater robot motion enhancement, and underwater object recognition and retrieval.
Realistic Sonar Image Simulation Using GAN
We have developed a method to synthesize realistic sonar images using a Generative Adversarial Network (GAN). A ray-tracing-based sonar simulator calculates semantic information of a viewed scene, and the GAN-based style transfer algorithm then generates realistic sonar images from the simulated images.
Deep-Learning-Based Crosstalk Removal in Forward Scan Sonar for Object Detection
We developed the detection and removal of crosstalk noise using a convolutional neural network in the images of forward scan sonar. Also, we applied the method to a three-dimensional point cloud generation and generated a more accurate point cloud.
Water Surface Lidar SLAM
This study explores the fusion of LiDAR and sonar sensors for mapping purposes in both surface and underwater environments. By acquiring data with LiDAR sensors on the surface and sonar sensors underwater, the goal is to improve mapping accuracy and coverage across both domains through integrated sensor data.
Underwater 3D Reconstruction
Underwater sonar and vision-based 3D shape reconstruction methods. The methods are used to localize and recognize underwater objects and generate large-scale underwater maps.
Social Contribution
Safety & Health Management System for Haenyeo
As part of its social contribution, we developed safety equipment from 2019 to 2023 to prevent the cultural extinction caused by the aging of Haenyeo (Korean female divers). The Smart Taewak/Homi Safety System utilizes low-cost sensors to manage the working conditions of haenyeo and to provide alerts to the surroundings in case of emergencies.
Self-Powered Monitoring/Sensing System for Offshore Aquaculture and AI-Based Real-Time Recognition Technology
We developed automation technology/equipment for reducing social costs of the primary industries through automated fish farming from 2016 to 2021. Underwater cameras, capable of observing fish species in extreme offshore aquaculture environments, are integrated with solar-powered buoy systems, enabling the acquisition of fish and environmental data through aquaculture monitoring.
