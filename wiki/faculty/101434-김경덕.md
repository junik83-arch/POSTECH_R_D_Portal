> 김경덕 교수는 포항공과대학교(POSTECH)에서 통합전산재료공학 연구실(ICMEL, Integrated Computational Materials Engineering Laboratory)을 이끌며, 친환경소재대학원(GIFT)과 연계된 연구·교육 활동을 수행하고 있습니다. 인공지능과 전산모사를 기반으로 금속, 이차전지, 반도체 소재를 설계하며, 머신러닝, 상장(phase-field) 모델링, 제일원리 계산, 이론 모델 개발, 실험을 아우르는 방법론으로 소재 내 상변화와 미세조직을 분석합니다. 대표 연구 과제로는 포스코홀딩스 미래기술연구원 AI연구소의 위탁을 받은 이차전지 양극재(NCM 등) 소성 공정 미세조직 최적화, 한국산업기술진흥원(포스코홀딩스·삼성SDI) 위탁의 원자 수준 배터리 양극 설계, 그리고 적층제조 소재의 확산·미세조직 및 기계적 물성 예측 연구가 있습니다. 주요 성과로는 Acta Materialia, Materials & Design, Scripta Materialia, Advanced Science 등에 게재된 알루미늄 합금 석출물, Ni기 초내열합금 IN738LC, Li-rich 층상 양극재 관련 논문들이 있으며, 이차전지 양극재 입자 성장 예측, 적층제조 IN738LC 열처리 미세조직 설계, 비등방성 입자 형상 예측 방법 등 특허도 출원했습니다. 또한 2026년 부산에서 열린 제3회 iHSM 국제학회에 초청연사로 참여해 발표했습니다.

## 연구관심분야
- Multiscale modeling of phase transformations in metals and alloys
- Computational design and discovery of new materials
- High-throughput discovery of new materials
- Machine-learning-assisted materials discovery

## 연구키워드
Computational thermodynamics, Computational kinetics, First-principles calculation, Phase-field model, Machine-learning-based material design

## 주요성과
- Development of multiscale modeling method using first-principles calculation and phase-field model
- Development of theoretical models for coarsening prediction in metal alloys
- Design structural metal alloys based on Integrated Computational Materials Engineering (ICME)
- Elucidating oxidation kinetic mechanism between oxide and metal during corrosion

Battery Microstructure Optimization
We are performing the large-scale simulations to predict cathode (e.g., NCM cathode) microstructure evolution with time and temperature during the sintering process.
The ultimate goal of this project is to investigate the underlying physics behind the sintering phase transformation based on thermodynamics and kinetics for high-performance energy storage battery cathode.
위탁기관) 포스코홀딩스 미래기술연구원 AI연구소
Atomic-Scale Battery Design
We are performing an atomic-scale analysis of the energetics of the cathode (e.g., Li- and Mn-rich layered structure cathode) to understand thermodynamic stability depends on crystal structure and composition.
The ultimate goal of this project is to optimize the high voltage, long lifespan, and superior mechanical stability in designing advanced cathode materials for the next-generation energy source.
위탁기관) 한국산업기술진흥원 (포스코홀딩스, 삼성SDI)
Additive Manufacturing
We are performing an atomic-scale analysis of solute diffusion, the large-scale simulations of microstructure evolution, and machine-learning-assisted model development for mechanical property prediction.
The ultimate goal of this project is to design new Additive Manufacturing (AM) structural materials with high strength and high thermal resistance for the next-generation manufacturing landscape.
위탁기관) 국방부 방위사업청, 한국연구재단
Electrical Steel
We are performing the experimental characterization and the large-scale simulations to understand microstructure evolution in steels (e.g., electrical steel).
The ultimate goal of this project is to investigate the mechanism of phase transformations (e.g., recrystallization, grain growth) and achieve high magnetic properties (minimum iron loss) of electrical steels for high performance of transformers, electric motors, and generators.
