## 주요성과
- Developed Lagrangian Floer theory of toric manifolds and orbifolds
- Developed a functorial formalism to understand homological mirror symmetry (Localized mirror functor and its non-commutative version, local to global constructions)
- Proved various mirror symmetry phenomenons involving orbifolds and/or its creptant resolution.
- New approach to study symplectic geometry of singularity.
