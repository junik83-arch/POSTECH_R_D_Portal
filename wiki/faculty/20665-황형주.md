> 황형주 교수는 POSTECH 수학과 교수로, Brown University에서 편미분방정식(PDE) 분야 박사학위를 받은 뒤 막스플랑크연구소, 듀크대, 트리니티칼리지더블린을 거쳐 2006년부터 POSTECH에 재직 중이며, 권경환 석좌교수(2020.09~2023.08), 포스텍 수리응용센터(PCAM) 센터장, BK21 FOUR POSTECH 수학 교육연구단장, 블록체인기술연구센터 부센터장 등을 맡고 있습니다. 연구 분야는 데이터사이언스와 수학적 인공지능, 뉴럴 미분방정식, ODE·PDE·확률 모형을 활용한 수리모델링, 응용해석학, 운동론(kinetic theory)과 유체, 수리생물학입니다. 연구실(AMSquare)은 포스코, 삼성전자, 더존비즈온 등과 산업 AI 과제(전이학습 기반 공정 모델 확산, 설비 이상 예측, 품질 예측, 부도율 예측 등)를 수행했고, 공장자동화 제어기 역공학, 지식 전이 모델, 블록체인 SNS 봇 탐지 등의 특허를 출원·등록했습니다. 또한 운동론 방정식의 심층신경망 해법, COVID-19 확산 수리모델, 파산 예측 모델의 설명가능성 등에 관한 논문과 초청강연 실적이 있으며, ReaDiNet, KSIAM 등 다수 학술대회를 조직하고 KRM, NHM 등 학술지 편집위원으로 활동했습니다.

riboHMM*: Comprehensive annotation of translated regions using ribosome footprint profiling data  – AMSquare
구분
[수리응용센터] Biostatistics 세미나
Title
riboHMM*: Comprehensive annotation of translated regions using ribosome footprint profiling data
발표자
심희정 교수(University of Melbourne)
일시
2019.07.01(월) 16:00
[수리응용센터] Seminar on Biostatistics
일시: 2019년 7월 1일(월) 16:00~
장소: 수리과학관 313호
발표자: 심희정 교수(University of Melbourne)
제목: riboHMM*: Comprehensive annotation of translated regions using ribosome footprint profiling data
초록:
Understanding the functional effects of gene
expression critically depends on the accurate and comprehensive annotation of
sequence elements which are translated in each gene. Ribosome profiling
provides direct and genome-wide measurements of translation levels in a given
cell type. In this talk, I will first introduce a method, riboHMM, that 1)
models a codon periodicity structure in ribosome profiling data, and 2)
integrates RNA sequence information and transcript expressions to identify
translated regions in a transcript. Applying riboHMM on ribosome profiling data
collected from human lymphoblastoid cell lines, we identified 7273 novel
translated regions, including 2442 translated upstream open reading frames (
uORFs
) and 2551 coding sequences from
transcripts that were previously annotated as non-coding. We observed that more
than 60% of the novel coding sequences use non-canonical start codons. We also
observed that ~40% of the 2442 translated
uORFs
are likely to regulate the
translation of their downstream coding regions.
Motivated by this observation, I will briefly
introduce another method, riboHMM2, for annotating a comprehensive set of
translated
uORFs
by
jointly modelling the fine-scale structure in ribosome profiling data around
translated
uORFs
and
downstream coding regions. While the previous riboHMM was able to search for
translated
uORFs
only
in the transcripts with translated downstream coding regions, riboHMM2 enables
annotation of translated
uORFs
in
the entire transcripts. It also allows us to infer the regulatory impact
of
uORFs
on
downstream coding regions (e.g., suppression), which is useful for gene
regulation studies to understand the mechanisms of
uORF
actions.

Title
Stochastic neural architecture search and its application to medical images
발표자
일시
Title: Stochastic neural architecture search and its application to medical images
Speaker: Dr. Leo Lim(Kakao Brain)
​
Date: 4pm, March. 7, 2019
Place: Math. Bldg. Rm. 404
Abstract:
Neural Architecture Search (NAS) algorithms lately attract attention from machine learning society due to their potential for Auto ML. The initial NAS algorithms employ reinforcement learning (RL) method which is powerful, but computationally expensive. In this lecture, it will be introduced that recent researches on NAS which are based on continuous relaxation e.g. Gumbel-softmax without using the RL method. We also verify the transferability of searched architecture by its application to medical images which consist of different modalities and organs.
