> 채민우 교수는 POSTECH 산업경영공학과 소속 교수로, 2019년 6월부터 재직하며 Statistics & Data Science(SDS) 연구실을 이끌고 있습니다. POSTECH 수학과 학사, 서울대학교 통계학 박사(지도교수 김용대) 학위를 받은 뒤 UT Austin 수학과에서 박사후연구원으로, 케이스웨스턴리저브대학교(CWRU) 수학·응용수학·통계학과에서 조교수로 근무한 이력이 있습니다. 연구 분야는 고차원·복잡 데이터 분석을 위한 통계적 방법론과 그 이론적 토대로, 딥러닝에 대한 통계적 관점, 분포적 강건 추론, 베이지안 온라인 학습과 도메인 적응, 베이지안 절차의 빈도주의 이론 등을 주요 주제로 삼고 있습니다. 최근 연구로는 표 형태 기반모형의 부분집단 분포 변화에 대한 강건 적응, 확산모형을 활용한 비모수 무방향 그래프 모형 선택, 일회 처리 환경에서의 베이지안 온라인 학습 등이 있으며, 의료 데이터 기반 공동연구에도 참여하고 있습니다. 학술 활동으로는 2025년부터 Bayesian Analysis 부편집장을 맡고 있고, 이전에는 Statistics 부편집장으로 활동했으며, 운용과학, 통계적 데이터마이닝, 베이지안 통계, 희소성 기반 통계 방법론, 데이터 사이언스를 위한 수학 등의 과목을 강의하고 있습니다.

Research Interests
Our research broadly lies in statistics and data science. We aim to develop principled statistical methods for analyzing high-dimensional and complex data, along with foundational theories to understand and justify these methods. Our interests span both methodological development and theoretical analysis, often guided by practical challenges arising in real-world applications.
Some of our current research directions include:
Statistical perspectives on deep learning
Distributionally robust inference
Bayesian online learning and domain adaptation
Frequentist theory for Bayesian procedures
We strive to maintain a balance between theoretical and applied research. Much of our applied work is driven by collaborations with researchers in both academia and industry. Below are a few notable recent collaborations:
Collaboration with
Seungho Song
: We are collaborating with Professor Seungho Song, a colorectal surgeon at Kyungpook National University School of Medicine, to model the risk of postoperative complications following colorectal cancer surgery. A key aspect of this project is leveraging datasets from different domains, such as surgical data from Korea and Japan, to enable effective modeling even in regions with limited data. The goal is to build robust and generalizable models that can support clinical decision-making in diverse healthcare settings.
Collaboration with Yogiyo and
Dong Gu Choi
: South Korea’s food delivery industry has experienced rapid growth over the past decade, presenting a range of interesting data science challenges. We collaborated with Yogiyo, one of the country’s largest food delivery companies, to address some of these problems. Our main focus was on predicting demand for food delivery, with the goal of informing the dynamic pricing of rider commissions. The collaboration itself was also featured in
media coverage
.
Collaboration with Samsung C&T and
Minseok Song
: We collaborated with Samsung C&T to develop a fashion recommendation system for SSF Shop. The project involved utilizing various data sources, including customer click history, product images, and a curated set of compatible fashion items collected by domain experts. The collaboration was also featured in
media coverage
.

How to Join
For Prospective Students
Thank you for your interest in our lab. If I have not replied to your message, I apologize. Unfortunately, I am not always able to respond to every inquiry individually. To learn more about our research, please explore the information provided on this website.
Please note that I cannot guarantee admission to the lab. The number of available positions depends on various factors such as funding and space, and all graduate admissions are managed centrally by the department rather than by individual faculty members.
For the Ph.D. program, I normally select students after observing their work during the two-year master’s program. Exceptions are made only in rare cases, for example when a student is exceptionally qualified or when there is a prearranged research collaboration or external funding.
For the master’s program, I currently accept students only through POSTECH’s Industrial Data Science (IDS) program, except in rare cases involving exceptional qualifications or special arrangements.
Undergraduate research opportunities are available only to Postechians. If you are a Postechian, you are welcome to visit my office, with or without an appointment.
Recommended Background
If you would like to join our lab, you should have a strong background in statistics, data science, machine learning, or a related field. This includes undergraduate-level knowledge of linear algebra, probability theory, deep learning, computer programming, and similar topics.
In addition, if you are interested in the Ph.D. program, a strong background in mathematical analysis and mathematical statistics is highly recommended.
