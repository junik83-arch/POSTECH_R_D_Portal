> 윤용주 교수는 POSTECH 화학공학과 소속으로 Advanced Nanocatalyst Design Lab(ANDL)을 이끄는 책임교수(PI)이며, 불균일계 촉매 분야에서 기초 과학과 산업적 응용을 연결하는 연구를 수행하고 있습니다. 연구실의 주요 주제는 화학제품 생산을 위한 선택적 수소화·탈수소화 반응, 청정수소 생산 및 저장, 그리고 데이터 기반 불균일 촉매 최적화 세 가지로 구성되어 있습니다. 구체적으로는 키랄 화합물 생산을 위한 거울상선택적 촉매와 표면 개질, CO2를 활용한 알칸 탈수소화, 암모니아 합성·분해 촉매와 전기화학적 질산염 환원, 메탄 열분해를 통한 터퀴즈 수소 및 탄소 부산물 생산 등을 다루며, in-situ 분광 분석과 반응속도 연구를 함께 활용합니다. 또한 실험·문헌 데이터베이스와 머신러닝을 결합해 촉매 성능을 예측·설계하고, AspenTech와 MATLAB 등 공정 시뮬레이션으로 산업적 적용성과 경제성·환경 영향을 평가합니다. 최근에는 ACS Catalysis, Journal of Materials Chemistry A, Journal of Catalysis, Applied Catalysis A: General, Catalysis Today, International Journal of Hydrogen Energy 등에 교신저자로 다수의 논문을 발표했습니다.

The
Advanced Nanocatalyst Design Lab (ANDL)
at POSTECH  is committed to advancing the frontiers of heterogeneous catalysis through innovation and interdisciplinary collaboration. Our research philosophy focuses on bridging fundamental science with practical applications to develop catalysts that address both current and future challenges in the energy and chemical industries.
By integrating advanced synthesis techniques, cutting-edge surface science and data-driven methodologies, we uncover the intricate relationships between catalyst structure, composition and performance. Our goal is not only to improve catalytic efficiency and selectivity but also to contribute to global sustainability by developing environmentally friendly processes. Learn more about our research on our dedicated page.

The
Advanced Nanocatalyst Design Lab (ANDL)
at POSTECH  is committed to advancing the frontiers of heterogeneous catalysis through innovation and interdisciplinary collaboration. Our research philosophy focuses on bridging fundamental science with practical applications to develop catalysts that address both current and future challenges in the energy and chemical industries.
By integrating advanced synthesis techniques, cutting-edge surface science and data-driven methodologies, we uncover the intricate relationships between catalyst structure, composition and performance. Our goal is not only to improve catalytic efficiency and selectivity but also to contribute to global sustainability by developing environmentally friendly processes. Learn more about our research on our dedicated page.

Selective hydrogenation / dehydrogenation for chemical production
■ Our research group develops enantioselective heterogeneous catalysts for the efficient production of chiral compounds essential to
the fine chemical and pharmaceutical industries. We explore surface modifications to tune structural and electronic properties for enhanced
enantioselectivity in C=O and C=C hydrogenation. We employ in-depth IR analysis to investigate surface reactions, providing mechanistic insights that guide catalyst optimization.
Through this approach, we aim to enable the efficient production of enantiomerically pure chiral compounds.
■ We develops innovative catalytic materials to efficiently convert alkanes into value-added chemicals while utilizing CO₂ in CO₂-assisted dehydrogenation.
We integrate reaction kinetics with advanced in-situ analysis to elucidate reaction mechanisms at the molecular level. By combining cutting-edge analytical tools with fundamental research,
we aim to design highly efficient and selective catalysts that drive the sustainable production of value-added chemicals while contributing to global CO₂ mitigation efforts.

Clean hydrogen production & storage
■ Our lab advances efficient hydrogen production and storage through the development of novel catalysts for ammonia synthesis and decomposition.
We investigate the electrochemical nitrate reduction reaction (NO
3
-RR) as a sustainable pathway for ammonia synthesis.
For ammonia decomposition, we design thermocatalysts and employ in-situ analytical techniques to identify key factors influencing catalytic performance.
By optimizing both green ammonia production and decomposition, our research contributes to a more sustainable hydrogen economy.
■ We develops advanced catalysts for methane pyrolysis to produce turquoise hydrogen and enhance the quality and yield of carbon co-products.
We also conduct in-depth reaction kinetic studies and modeling to optimize process conditions and elucidate fundamental reaction mechanisms for maximum efficiency.
Through these efforts, we strive to develop scalable and energy-efficient catalytic processes that drive the sustainable production of clean hydrogen and carbon precursors for high-value applications.

Data-driven optimization for heterogeneous catalysis
■
Our lab aims to optimize heterogeneous catalysis by integrating machine learning with experimental and literature data.
We build a robust experimental database and leverage machine learning models to capture relationships between catalyst performance and both design and operating parameters.
This data-driven approach enables catalyst performance prediction and design, minimizing experimental trials and accelerating optimal catalyst discovery.
■
We leverages process simulation to evaluate the industrial applicability of catalytic processes.
We use tools such as AspenTech and MATLAB to optimize reaction conditions, improve process efficiency, and assess economic and environmental impacts.
This approach enables the design and evaluation of catalysts and processes that maximize sustainability and energy efficiency in industrial applications.
