> 박규영 교수는 POSTECH 친환경소재대학원 소속으로, Energy Materials Research Laboratory(EMRL, 철강에너지 소재 연구실)를 이끄는 책임교수입니다. 연구실은 리튬·소듐 이차전지의 양극 및 음극 소재를 원자 수준, 입자 수준, 전극 수준에서 연구하며, 리튬·소듐·칼륨 이온 기반 이차전지의 작동 원리 규명과 고효율 에너지 저장 시스템 설계, 소재의 전기화학, 방사광 등 첨단 광원을 활용한 소재 분석을 주요 주제로 삼고 있습니다. 홈페이지에는 논문 95건과 특허 20건이 등재되어 있으며, 리튬과잉 층상 산화물의 경로 의존적 규칙-불규칙 상 변화를 다룬 Energy & Environmental Science 논문(2026, 교신저자, Inside Front Cover 선정)과 고전압 단결정 미드니켈 층상 양극의 용량 퇴화 기구를 분석한 Journal of Power Sources 논문 등이 포함됩니다. 특허로는 리튬이온전지용 고용량 미드니켈 양극 소재, 열폭주가 저감된 양극 전극 제조법, 건식 공정용 복합 양극 활물질 등이 있으며, 연구실은 건식전극 소재 섬유화 장비와 드라이룸, 다수의 충방전 평가 설비를 보유하고 있습니다.

## 연구관심분야
- Energy storage materials/Li- and Na-ion batteries
- Material and battery system processing
- Material analysis - synchrotron based, Calculation

## 연구키워드

## 주요성과
- Development of new active materials for Li-/Na-ion batteries
- Fine crystal & electron structure analysis for energy storage materials
- Electrochemical system for energy storage materials
- Processing engineering for high energy density system
- Battery recyclings

Rechargeable battery systems
Efficient generation and transportation of energy is now the most important task of our time. Rechargeable battery systems using lithium-, sodium- or potassium-ions have received a great deal of attention in industry as well as academia due to their high energy density and reliability, but there are still some issues to be solved. In our laboratory, we are investigating the operating principle of batteries and researching to design more efficient energy storage systems.
Material Science & Energy Storage
The "active material" of the electrode primarily determines the energy density, power density, cycle life, and safety of the battery. So we can say that understanding the basics of active materials is of the utmost importance! Here we design and find next-generation materials to create a clean future.
Electrochemistry of Materials
The electrochemical reaction from the material is too complicated. We need to not only understand the charge transfer at the surface but also consider the ion diffusion in the solid. In addition, since the battery system is made up of many-body particle electrodes, unforeseen phenomena may be discovered. We understand the basics of electrochemical reactions.
Material Analysis using Advanced Light Source
How can we explore materials? In our lab, we use a variety of beam sources such as X-rays and neutrons to precisely analyze materials at the atomic level. Discover world-class analytics technology
