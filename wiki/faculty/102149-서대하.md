- Single-molecule biophysics of receptor signaling and protein interactions
- Nanoparticle synthesis and functional design for catalysis and therapeutics
- Visualization and control of chemical reactions at the single-molecule level Nanomedicine and targeted drug delivery using nanoparticles and exosomes
