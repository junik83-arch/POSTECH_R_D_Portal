> 김현우 교수는 포항공과대학교(POSTECH) 화학과 부교수로, 지속가능 유기반응 연구실(SC&C Lab)을 이끌며 광화학·전기화학을 활용한 유기합성, 라디칼 유기화학, 유기 전기합성을 연구하고 있으며, 전기화학적 거동에 대한 이해를 유기 전해질·용매 등 유기 기반 배터리 소재 개발로 확장하고 있습니다. KAIST에서 학사(2013)와 장석복 교수 지도로 박사학위(2018)를 받은 뒤 기초과학연구원(IBS) 연구위원, 코넬대학교에서 Song Lin·Tristan Lambert 교수와 박사후연구원을 거쳐 2020년 이화여자대학교 조교수로 임용되었고 2022년부터 POSTECH에 재직 중입니다. 알켄의 전기촉매적 수소관능화, 구리 전기촉매를 이용한 비스(디플루오로메틸화), 광 유도 중간체 산화를 활용한 전기합성 등을 교신저자로 JACS, Angew. Chem., Nature Communications, ACS Catalysis 등에 발표했습니다. 수상 실적으로는 대한화학회 제19회 젊은 유기화학자상(2025), 일본화학회 전기화학 분야 Distinguished Lectureship Award(2025), 삼성미래기술육성사업 선정(2024), Thieme Chemistry Journals Award(2024), 제13회 포스코사이언스펠로십(2022) 등이 있습니다.

Brief introduction to our research field
(NAS-KAST symposium)
In our ongoing research, we are dedicated to advancing the realms of photochemical and electrochemical organic synthesis. Our primary goal is to redefine photo- and electrocatalysis, enabling the efficient synthesis of value-added molecules from raw chemical feedstock.
​​
We endeavor to pioneer a fresh conceptual framework,
pushing the boundaries of both photochemical and electrochemical synthetic organic methodologies.
Through our exploration of innovative approaches, we strive to contribute to the creation of sustainable and economically feasible pathways in organic synthesis, utilizing
state-of-the-art photochemical and electrocatalytic techniques
.
​
Furthermore, we are concurrently conducting research that applies insights gleaned from the electrochemical behavior of organic and organometallic molecules observed in these processes to the development of
organic-based battery materials such as organic electrolytes and solvents.

Hyunwoo Kim received his B.S. degree in Chemistry from Korea Advanced Institute of Science and Technology (KAIST) in 2013 and earned his Ph.D. in 2018 under the supervision of Prof. Sukbok Chang at the same institution. He then worked as a Research Fellow at the Institute for Basic Science (IBS) for one year before completing his postdoctoral research at Cornell University with Profs. Song Lin and Tristan Lambert in the Department of Chemistry and Chemical Biology. In 2020, he joined Ewha Womans University as an Assistant Professor in the Department of Chemistry and NanoScience. Since 2022, he has been a faculty member in the Department of Chemistry at POSTECH, where his research focuses on radical organic chemistry and organic electrosynthesis, aiming to develop sustainable synthetic methodologies.
